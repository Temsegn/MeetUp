01 Introduction

Samtal is a conferencing workspace for organizations. After signing in, members work inside one shared application that brings together meetings, calendar, contacts, messages, recordings, templates, reports, rooms, billing, and workspace settings.

This guide explains essential workflows using the exact labels that appear in the product, so you can follow along on screen as you read.

What you will learn

Account & Navigation

Create an account, sign in, and find your way around the Dashboard and sidebar.

Meetings

Start Instant or Scheduled meetings from New Meeting, then join the live room.

Workspace Administration

Invite users, create rooms, and manage Billing & Plan upgrades with payment.

Personal Settings

Update profile, notifications, and account preferences from Settings.

02 Getting Started

You need a supported desktop browser, a valid email address, and network access to your organization’s Samtal site. A workspace invitation may be required before you can join.

Recommended first path

  1. 1Create an account (Sign up) or accept an invite.
  2. 2Sign in with your email and password.
  3. 3Open the Dashboard and explore the sidebar.
  4. 4Create a meeting with New Meeting.

Primary navigation

03 Sign Up / Sign In

1. Create an account

Open Get started or go to /auth/sign-up. Enter first name, last name, work email, and password. Optionally add a company name and team size. Accept the Terms, then click Create Account.

Figure 1 — Sign up

2. Sign in to your workspace

Open Sign in or go to /auth. Enter your email and password. Optionally keep Remember me selected, then click Sign in. A successful sign-in takes you to the Dashboard.

Figure 2 — Sign in
Notes — Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page if you need a reset link. Google sign-in appears when it is configured on the server; Microsoft sign-in may show as unavailable until configured.

04 Dashboard

After signing in, the Dashboard opens automatically. From here you see a welcome message, meeting summaries, insights, calendar, and quick actions. Use the sidebar to move between modules.

Figure 3 — Dashboard
New Meeting. Use the header control New Meeting to start an Instant meeting or create a Scheduled meeting. Choose Create & join for an instant room, or Create meeting for a scheduled one. Full click paths are in the next section.

05 New Meeting — Instant & Scheduled

Meetings start from the blue New Meeting button in the top header. It is available from almost every signed-in page.

Exact click path
  1. Click New Meeting in the header.
  2. The menu opens with two choices: Instant meeting and Scheduled meeting.
  3. Pick one — each opens its own dialog with the fields shown below.
  4. Fill the form, then confirm with Create & join or Create meeting.

Step A — Open the New Meeting menu

Click New Meeting. The dropdown shows:

  • Instant meeting — “Add a title, then jump into the live room.”
  • Scheduled meeting — “Choose title, date, and participants.”
Figure 4 — New Meeting menu: Instant meeting and Scheduled meeting

Step B — Instant meeting

Choose Instant meeting. The dialog title is Start instant meeting.

  1. 1Enter a Title (required).
  2. 2Set Duration (minutes) if needed (default 30).
  3. 3Optionally add agenda items with + Add item.
  4. 4Optionally invite guests under Invite by email (guests), then Add.
  5. 5Click Create & join to enter the live room immediately (or Cancel).
Figure 5 — Start instant meeting dialog (Create & join)
Teammates. Workspace members on your teams are added automatically. Use guest email invites only for people outside your teams.

Step C — Scheduled meeting

Choose Scheduled meeting. The dialog title is Schedule a meeting.

  1. 1Enter a Title.
  2. 2Pick Date & time.
  3. 3Set Duration (minutes).
  4. 4Optionally add agenda topics and guest emails.
  5. 5Click Create meeting (not Create & join) to save it on the calendar.
Figure 6 — Schedule a meeting dialog (Create meeting)

Inside the live room

Use the control bar for Mic, Camera, Raise Hand, Screen, Participants, Chat, Whiteboard, and Leave. Guests may wait in a waiting room until a host admits them. Optional recording depends on plan permissions.

07 Invite & Add Users

Workspace owners and admins add people from Settings → Members (also reachable via Workspace).

Exact click path — add a new user
  1. Open the sidebar → Settings (or Workspace → Members).
  2. In the settings sections list, click Members.
  3. Click Invite Members (header also shows Create Member).
  4. You land on the Create User screen.
  5. Fill the form and click Create User.

Members list

Search members, filter by role (Owner, Admin, Member), and manage existing people from the table.

Figure 13 — Members list with Invite Members

Create User screen (what you fill in)

After clicking Invite Members, the Create User page shows:

  • User Information — Full Name *, Work Email *, optional phone, department, job title.
  • Role & Access — Role Member or Admin, optional Reports To, Default or Custom Access.
  • Send Invitation switch (on by default) — user receives an email invitation.
  • Right side: User Preview and Role & Permissions checklist.
  • Footer actions: Cancel or Create User.
Figure 14 — Create User / invite form (exact fields you click)

08 Create Room

Dedicated rooms are managed under Settings → Rooms. Use them for recurring spaces with their own capacity and permissions.

Exact click path — create a room
  1. Open Settings → Rooms.
  2. Click Create Room (page header or empty-state button).
  3. Fill Room Information, optional members, and Room Settings toggles.
  4. Click Create Room at the bottom (or top) of the form.

Rooms list

Figure 15 — Rooms list (Create Room)

Create Room form (fields you use)

  • Room Name *, Room ID *, Description, Capacity *, Room Type, Department, Tags, optional image.
  • Room Members — search and Add Member.
  • Room Settings toggles — Allow recording, Allow chat, Screen sharing, File sharing, Waiting room, Room approval.
  • Right side shows a live Room Preview.
Figure 16 — Create Room form (full fields and settings)

09 Upgrade & Payment

Plans control participant minutes, members, concurrent meetings, and recording storage. Free, Pro, and Enterprise are compared on Billing & Plan.

Exact click path — upgrade with payment
  1. Click sidebar Upgrade, or open Settings → Billing & Plan.
  2. On the summary, click Open full billing (or go to /app/billing).
  3. Compare Free / Pro / Enterprise cards.
  4. Click Pay $49.00 · Upgrade to Pro (or Enterprise).
  5. In the checkout dialog, enter card details and click Pay $49.00.

Billing summary (Settings)

Figure 17 — Billing & Plan summary with Open full billing

Full billing — plans

The full Billing page shows usage (Used / Included / Remaining / Overage), plan cards, Payment method (Add card), and invoices.

Figure 18 — Full billing with Pay · Upgrade buttons

Payment checkout dialog

After clicking an upgrade button, the Upgrade to Pro dialog asks for Name on card, Card number, Expiry, and CVC. Confirm with Pay $49.00 or dismiss with Cancel / Close.

Figure 19 — Upgrade payment checkout dialog
Demo checkout. In demo deployments, payment may be simulated with no real charge. Only brand, last 4 digits, and expiry are stored for the payment method on file.

13 Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I create a meeting?
Use New Meeting in the header from almost any signed-in page, or open Meetings in the sidebar. Choose Instant or Scheduled.
What is the difference between Create & join and Create meeting?
Create & join starts an Instant meeting and opens the live room now. Create meeting saves a Scheduled meeting for later.
How do I add a new user?
Settings → Members → Invite Members → fill Create User → Create User.
How do I create a room?
Settings → Rooms → Create Room → fill the form → Create Room.
How do I upgrade and pay?
Billing & Plan → Open full billing → Pay · Upgrade to Pro (or Enterprise) → enter card → Pay.
What is the waiting room?
Guests can wait until a host admits them. Hosts control admission from the live meeting.
Can I share my screen?
Yes. In the live room, use Screen on the control bar. Rooms can also require Screen sharing in Room Settings.
Where are recordings stored?
Open Recordings. Storage limits follow the organization plan under Billing & Plan.
What does AI Insights (Beta) mean?
It is an early feature. Availability and content may change as the product evolves.
